# Data research v0.10.3 — Valuable Card Knowledge Engine

Official manufacturer sources were used only to document card structure and chase-program identity. They are **not** used as price sources.

- Upper Deck 2025-26 Series 2 product page: documents Young Guns Exclusives /100, High Gloss /10, Clear Cut, Outburst Red /25 and Outburst Gold 1/1.
- Topps 2026 Chrome Premier League product page: identifies premium case pulls including Chrome Anime, Helix, Triple Platinum, Genius Chrome, Give Him His Flowers and Locked In.
- Topps 2025/26 Inception UCC product page: establishes the release as autograph/relic driven and includes autograph-patch content.
- Topps 2025/26 Museum UCC product page: confirms autograph, relic and autograph-relic box structure plus Museum Roll of Honor.

Guardrail: manufacturer language such as “premium”, “case pull”, “rare” or a documented print run may increase review priority, but it must never create a price, profit estimate, ROI or max bid. Actual market value must be supported by comparable market evidence.
