# FlipFynd v0.14.23

- Recognizes explicitly named Young Guns variants such as Canvas, Exclusives, High Gloss, Clear Cut and Outburst as research-priority candidates.
- Requires both the Young Guns program name and a documented variant term, so ordinary Young Guns and ordinary Canvas cards are not promoted by the new rule.
- The signal only prevents false-negative filtering: it creates neither market value nor a BUY decision, and exact checklist/card identity plus SOLD evidence remain mandatory.
- Primary references: Upper Deck's official [2023-24 Series 2 checklist](https://upperdeck.com/checklist/2023-24-upper-deck-series-2-checklist/) and [2025-26 Series 2 product documentation](https://upperdeck.com/2025-26-upper-deck-series-2/).
