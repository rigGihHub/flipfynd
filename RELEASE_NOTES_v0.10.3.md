# FlipFynd v0.10.3 — Valuable Card Knowledge Engine

- Adds a separate Valuable Card Knowledge Engine for identifying card archetypes that deserve extra value research: flagship/rookie programs, rookie autos, RPAs, SSP/case hits, low-numbered variants, 1/1s, autographs and relic/patch structures.
- Combines player demand, documented card structure, identity confidence and sold-comp evidence into a **research priority**, never a market price.
- Adds explicit market-evidence state: strong / some / limited / insufficient.
- Adds UI section with card archetype, research priority, structural score, evidence score and guardrails.
- Expands the official-source knowledge bank with current Young Guns Outburst Red /25 and Gold 1/1, Topps Chrome Premier League Chrome Anime and Locked In, Inception Autograph Patch and Museum Roll of Honor.
- Cache model bumped to `flip_v21_valuable_card_knowledge`.
