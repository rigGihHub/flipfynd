# FlipFynd v0.6.8 – card-market knowledge research

Purpose: improve recognition of potentially important hockey/football card structures without turning prestige into a price estimate.

## Rules
- This knowledge base is NOT a price guide.
- No chase/rarity signal creates market value on its own.
- Monetary value still requires comparable market evidence.
- Official manufacturer/checklist information is used to confirm product structure, rarity wording and named programs.

## Added hockey knowledge
- Upper Deck Young Guns: tracked as a flagship rookie-program signal.
- SP Authentic Future Watch / Future Watch Autographs: tracked as rookie/numbered-auto program signals.
- Future Watch Auto Patch / Limited Auto Material: tracked as premium numbered rookie patch-auto structures.
- The Cup Rookie Auto Patch: tracked as a premium numbered rookie structure.
- The Cup low-numbered/1-of-1 named parallels are recognized as scarcity signals, not automatic prices.

## Added football knowledge
- Topps Chrome UEFA: Helix and Munich at Night tracked as named ultra-rare chase inserts.
- Topps Chrome UEFA: Budapest at Night tracked as a named rare insert; Purple Mojo tracked as a low-numbered parallel signal.
- Marks of Excellence tracked as an autograph-program signal.
- Topps Merlin: numbered/refractor and Atomic Refractor structures recognized.
- Panini Prizm: Downtown, Color Blast and Manga tracked as named ultra-rare insert signals.
- Product recognition expanded for Topps Chrome Sapphire, Donruss Optic and Metal Universe.

## Important limitation
Manufacturer descriptions can establish that a program is rare/chase-oriented, but they do not prove the resale value of a specific card. FlipFynd therefore exposes these as identification/market-knowledge signals only and does not add a SEK value from them.
