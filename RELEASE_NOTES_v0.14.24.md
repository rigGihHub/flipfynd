# FlipFynd v0.14.24

- Adds a product-bound research signal for officially documented Topps football chase inserts, including Helix, Chrome Anime, European Tour, Berlin/Munich/Budapest at Night, Mindgame, Hype and Mojo.
- Requires both a Topps/UEFA/UCL/UCC/Premier League context and an exact named program, preventing generic words such as Helix or Hype from being promoted on their own.
- These names only protect promising listings from false-negative filtering. They never establish value or BUY; exact card identity and verified SOLD evidence remain mandatory.
